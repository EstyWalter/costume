Prop Design Essentials

Props tell stories. Focus on materials, scale, and texture to sell the illusion.

Material Map

  • Foam: lightweight for armor, blades, and sci-fi shapes.
  • Worbla & Thermoplastics: heat-formed, rigid finishes.
  • Resins: durable casts for repeated use or heroic props.
  • Fabric & EVA: flexible pieces for fast changes.

Paint & Weathering

  • Layer primers, base coats, and washes for depth.
  • Use dry-brushing to highlight edges and sculpted details.
  • Seal with matte clear coat to remove plastic sheen.
  • Add grime and chip effects where hands would touch.

Texture Toolkit

  • Sand, score, and heat foam for worn finishes.
  • Use fabric wraps for grip and authenticity.
  • Greebles + decals add scale and storytelling.
  • Experiment with airbrush gradients for realism.

Makeup & Prosthetic Workflow

A clean, layered process makes makeup last through long shoots or stage runs.

Character Makeup Steps

  1. Prep: cleanse, tone, and apply barrier spray.
  2. Correct: neutralize undertones with color theory.
  3. Build: cream base, then set with translucent powder.
  4. Detail: contour, highlight, and add story-driven marks.
  5. Seal: use fixers and mattifying sprays.

Prosthetic Application

  1. Test fit and trim edges before adhesive.
  2. Apply pros-aide or silicone adhesive in thin layers.
  3. Blend edges with alcohol-activated palettes.
  4. Match skin texture with stipple sponges.
  5. Finish with flexible sealers for movement.

Quick Tips for All-Day Wear

Keep a touch-up kit nearby with blotting paper, translucent powder, q-tips, and matching paints. For stage heat, pack a handheld fan and cooling mist.

45–60 minutes for base character makeup
2–4 hours for multi-piece prosthetics

Safety & Hygiene

Protect performers and preserve your tools with pro hygiene habits.

Skin Safety

  • Patch test adhesives 24 hours ahead.
  • Use disposable sponges and clean brushes daily.
  • Keep remover oils on set for emergencies.

Ventilation

  • Work in well-ventilated areas for resins and adhesives.
  • Wear respirators during sanding or airbrushing.
  • Store solvents in fire-safe containers.

Tool Care

  • Sanitize tools with 70% isopropyl alcohol.
  • Label products with open dates and expiry.
  • Keep a log of reusable prosthetic pieces.
